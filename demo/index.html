Follow-Up CRM: interactive demo

Status and dates become a daily list of reviewable actions. Rules are data, drafts are reviewed by a person.

Fictional data only. Every customer here is invented. This page runs entirely in your browser, connects to nothing, and stores nothing.

Try it: on Rules, change an offset and watch Today change. On Customers, change a status, then look for the superseded draft on Today. Approve a draft and see it recorded in the log.
